Autocall Hedging: LSV vs LV

Long spot skew, short forward skew, hedge P&L dynamics — how LV and LSV models differ for autocall hedging, SSR, and calibration.

01

Skew and Forward Skew under LV and LSV

Under LV\text{LV}LV, forward skew flattens significantly. Under LSV\text{LSV}LSV, forward skew is steeper than LV — but still flattens with tenor.

Forward smile comparison

Term structure of forward skew

◎Explanation

Under LSV, forward skew is steeper than LV. But even under LSV, it still flattens with tenor: 6m1y6\text{m}1\text{y}6m1y is flatter than 3m1y3\text{m}1\text{y}3m1y.

02

Why Is an Autocall Short Skew but Long Forward Skew?

Setup: effective life =1Y= 1Y=1Y, maturity T=3YT = 3YT=3Y. At t=1Mt = 1Mt=1M, the trader is already exposed to instantaneous forward skew. The forward skew 3M→1Y3M \to 1Y3M→1Y is steeper under LSV — forward-starting vanillas in the hedge cost more.

Vega and Vanna profile

Re-hedging dynamics

Vanna < 0 right of vega peak: when S↓S \downarrowS↓, vega ↑\uparrow↑ — trading gets longer vol on the way down. Must re-hedge by buying vol at higher levels. Under LSV this costs more (steeper forward skew). Exposure concentrated around the barrier.

Coupon offset

⚠Coupon effect — partial offset

KI barrier → short forward skew (trading). Snowball coupons → long forward skew (opposite sensitivity) (trading). Large coupons partially compensate → net LSV/LV gap shrinks.

LV overestimates SSR

⚠LV overprices SSR

Under LV: SSR→2\text{SSR} \to 2SSR→2 as T→0T \to 0T→0, SSR→3\text{SSR} \to 3SSR→3 as T→∞T \to \inftyT→∞. So SSRLV∈[2,3]\text{SSR}_{\text{LV}} \in [2, 3]SSRLV​∈[2,3] — systematically too high vs realized.

Chain of effects

  • SSR↑\text{SSR} \uparrowSSR↑ ⟹ fwd skew flattens ⟹ autocall cheaper
  • SSR↓\text{SSR} \downarrowSSR↓ ⟹ fwd skew steepens ⟹ autocall more expensive

Under LSV, ρ\rhoρ controls spot-vol. ρ\rhoρ lower → more SV → vol rises more when S↓S \downarrowS↓ → steeper fwd skew → autocall more expensive.

Limitations of flat correlation

⚠Limitation

A single ρˉ\bar{\rho}ρˉ​ does not capture the term structure of vol-of-vol. Realized ρ\rhoρ varies across tenors (concave curve) — flat ρˉ\bar{\rho}ρˉ​ overestimates at short dates, underestimates at long dates → mispricing at both ends.
